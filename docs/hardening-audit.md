## Checked and fine (no change proposed)

- **Service crash or hang:** the separate `--fan-guard` process restores BIOS fan control 3 s after the last heartbeat, or immediately when its pipe closes. The Windows service is set to restart twice.
- **Sensors vanish or stop updating:** that fan goes to 100% at once. After 3 failed reads in a row, every fan goes to 100%. Stale readings (over 2 s old) count as failures. The guard only gets a heartbeat on fresh readings.
- **Pump:** the hardware port forces header 7 to 100% on every write, and both `Configure` and `Hold` refuse header 7.
- **App or pipe drops:** fan control lives entirely in the service, so a disconnect changes nothing. The app reconnects with back-off (0.5 s up to 10 s) and shows "Service not running — showing the last known values".
- **OpenRGB not running:** the service retries every 5 s, logs once a minute, and the device list shows the devices as disconnected.
- **Lamp, router or air fryer offline:** each is in its own module with its own retry. A lamp switched off at the wall shows as offline, not as an error.
- **Pipe:** network access is denied, lines are capped at 64 KB, commands are limited to 20 a second per process, and every command validates its arguments. Unverified clients are read-only. Tokens, router passwords and sessions are stored in the `%ProgramData%` secret store (DPAPI LocalMachine plus entropy, admin-only ACL).
